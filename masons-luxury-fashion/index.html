MILANO

The Mason's Men's Milano Pants are the extra slim chinos of the Mason's family: two distinct versions in construction, one clean-cut and recognizable fit. The Milano Style has a clean and sartorial profile, while the Milano Jogger brings the same line onto a more contemporary and comfortable construction. The models are available in stretch gabardine and velvet, with a palette ranging from neutrals such as taupe, biscuit and grey to deeper shades such as midnight blue, forest green and dark red.
